Set up your squint

Each blue bar shows how open that eye is right now: empty is shut, full is wide open. Drag the handles under a bar to move its lines. To set it up:

  1. Squint and watch your squint eye's bar drop. Drag the orange handle so its line sits a little above where the bar drops to.
  2. Relax your eyes. Drag the purple handle so its line sits a little below where the bar rests. Passthrough turns off when the bar rises past it.
  3. Squint again and watch your other eye's bar. Drag the green handles so the band covers where that bar sits while you squint.

Then squint a few times. Squinting at the top should light up every time, and stay off when you blink or look around. The line under each bar tells you where it is right now.
